WHY YOUR VOTE MATTERS

Minecraft Story Mode was more than just a game. It was a journey filled with friendship, tough choices, and unforgettable moments with Jesse and the Order of the Stone. For many of us, it was the first time we truly felt like heroes in the world of Minecraft.

When Telltale Games shut down, the future of Minecraft Story Mode became uncertain. But the story never really ended. The passion from the community has only grown stronger over the years, with fans creating art, sharing memories, and keeping the spirit of the Order alive.

By reaching 100,000 supporters, we can send a powerful message to Telltale Games and Mojang that the demand for Minecraft Story Mode is still here. This isn't just about bringing back a game. It's about showing them that the stories we experienced together matter, and that there are still thousands of players who would love to see Jesse and the gang return for one more adventure.

Every single vote brings us closer to that goal. The Order of the Stone stood together against the Wither Storm, against the Admin, and against impossible odds. Now we need to stand together again. This is our chance to make our voices heard and prove that the legacy of Minecraft Story Mode deserves to continue.
